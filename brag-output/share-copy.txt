LexPen: type markdown, get the page, Mermaid and math included, and fenced SQL, C#, JSON or PowerShell coloured as their own language. Completion and indentation as you type; F12 to the definition, Ctrl+G, block to block, move a block, Reformat that lines up C# and SQL clauses and joins. Occurrences, find, highlights with notes that print in the margin. Many cursors, column mode, CSV as live columns, macros on your own ribbon, compare, PDF and hex; every setting searchable, themes from parchment to ink. Open source, for Windows: github.com/ArcoW2/LexPen
